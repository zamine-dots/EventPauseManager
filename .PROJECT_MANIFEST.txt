🎯 TYPICAL WORKFLOW

Before Event:
1. Add all participants via admin dashboard
2. Click "Generate QR Codes"
3. Download and print QR codes
4. Set up scanning station

During Event:
1. Staff scans QR code with phone
2. Scanner page opens automatically
3. Click "Mark Food Taken"
4. Click "Mark Coffee Taken"
5. Dashboard updates in real-time

After Event:
1. Click "Export CSV"
2. Click "Reset Status"
3. Ready for next event!
